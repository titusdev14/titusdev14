Biotech story or smth

So anyway, in the year 2096, climate change got so bad that the heat trapped more radiation in the atmosphere, and it created life. The Earth got "flesh tumors" or something, and they could regenerate themselves and split if given sunlight, which it fed off of through photosynthesis.

These flesh tumors could become actually sentient, apparently. So naturally, intelligent life was made out of them. And like everything else, the first thought on humanity's mind was, "how do i weaponize/profit from this?" with no regard for ethics. So the world fought over the tumors, with nations with the tumors being able to mine them and create an infinite, regenerating amount of soldiers to fight for them in WW3. As the USA was cornered, it launched nuclear weapons, resulting in a nuclear exchange and the collapse of civilization.
